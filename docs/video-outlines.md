# Recording outlines — drafts, not transcripts
Record your actual understanding and experience. Do not claim a personal learning change or verification you have not performed.

Every video opens with two statements, after your review makes them true:
1. "I used ChatGPT/Codex for implementation, validation and drafting. I reviewed the work; the analysis and decision are mine."
2. "This is education, not investment advice."

## Video 1 — at most 3 minutes
- 0:00–0:20: opening statements and decision question.
- 0:20–1:10: explain how your actual thinking changed from Lab 15 to a rejection after stability testing. Describe only changes you genuinely understand and endorse.
- 1:10–2:10: show one AI output you checked: independently recompute mean times 12 less fees or verify the reserve calculation. Show your actual check.
- 2:10–2:50: limits, remaining policy questions and final ownership.

## Video 2 — at most 5 minutes
- 0:00–0:20: statements.
- 0:20–1:00: README installation and `prices.csv` boundary.
- 1:00–2:00: `load_prices`, `signal`, `estimates`; distinguish observation and availability dates.
- 2:00–3:20: `measure` and `run`; explain exhaustive grid, fully invested weights, return floor, buffer, signal cap and minimum volatility.
- 3:20–4:20: `stress_suite`, infeasible case and stop condition; show the actual locked record once completed.
- 4:20–4:50: app controls and limitations.

## Video 3 — at most 5 minutes
Open with the app already running live, while giving the statements.
- 0:00–1:15: committee ask: reject live adoption, allocation range 0–0%; show all portfolio verdicts.
- 1:15–2:20: base allocation and window sensitivity in the live app.
- 2:20–3:20: signal data card, estimation uncertainty and missing policy.
- 3:20–4:20: monitoring and conditions for reconsideration.
- 4:20–4:50: restate the decision and committee action.
